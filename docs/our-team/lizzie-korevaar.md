# Dr Lizzie Korevaar

![Dr Lizzie Korevaar](../images/lizzie-korevaar.jpg){ .person }

Lizzie Korevaar is a Research Fellow in Biostatistics at the University of Melbourne. She completed her PhD in 2023 at Monash University. Lizzie provides statistical expertise to trials of pregnant women living in malaria endemic countries in her work as part of MISCH. She also conducts statistical methods research, which focuses on evaluating statistical for meta-analysis of rare outcomes and interrupted time series studies, and to design and analyse cluster-randomized trials. She is currently co-convenor of the Cochrane Statistical Methods Group and co-chair of the Statistical Society of Australia’s Mentoring Program.
