# Megan Hawkins

![Megan Hawkins](../images/megan-hawkins.jpg){ .person }

Megan is a biostatistician based at the Peter Doherty Institute for Infection and Immunity, The University of Melbourne. She provides statistical support to clinical and laboratory researchers across infection and immunity studies, with expertise in clinical trials, observational studies, and immunological data analysis. Megan works on individual participant data meta-analyses that assess how parasite clearance in Plasmodium vivax changes with the use and dose of primaquine, and with different schizonticidal antimalarials.
